## System Requirements

### Minimum Requirements
- **Browser:** Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
- **Internet:** Stable broadband connection (minimum 1 Mbps)
- **Screen Resolution:** 1024x768 (tablet and desktop optimized)
- **JavaScript:** Must be enabled

### Recommended Requirements
- **Browser:** Latest version of Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
- **Internet:** High-speed connection (5+ Mbps) for optimal performance
- **Screen Resolution:** 1920x1080 or higher
- **RAM:** 4GB+ for smooth multitasking

### Mobile Support
- **iOS:** 12.0 or later (Safari, Chrome)
- **Android:** 8.0 or later (Chrome, Firefox)
- **Responsive design** works on tablets and phones

## Account Setup

### New User Registration

1. **Receive Invitation Email**
   - Check your email for an invitation from your organization
   - Click the "Accept Invitation" link
   - The link expires after 7 days

2. **Create Your Account**
   - Enter your personal information:
     - Full name
     - Email address (must match invitation)
     - Phone number
     - Preferred language (Turkish/English)
   - Create a strong password:
     - Minimum 8 characters
     - Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers
     - Include special characters (!@#$%^&*)

3. **Verify Your Email**
   - Check for verification email
   - Click verification link
   - Return to login page

### Password Requirements

Your password must include:
- ✅ At least 8 characters
- ✅ One uppercase letter (A-Z)
- ✅ One lowercase letter (a-z)
- ✅ One number (0-9)
- ✅ One special character (!@#$%^&*)

**Example of strong password:** `MyBDC2024!`

## First Login

### Login Process

1. **Navigate to Platform**
   - Open your web browser
   - Go to your organization's BDC platform URL
   - Bookmark the page for easy access

2. **Enter Credentials**
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Select "Remember me" for trusted devices

3. **Two-Factor Authentication (if enabled)**
   - Enter verification code from SMS or authenticator app
   - Save backup codes in a secure location

### Forgot Password?

1. Click "Forgot Password?" on login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check email for reset instructions
4. Follow link to create new password
5. Use new password to log in

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your central hub for all BDC platform activities. Here's what you'll see:

### Main Statistics Cards

**For Students/Beneficiaries:**
- **Overall Progress:** Your completion percentage across all programs
- **Active Programs:** Number of programs you're currently enrolled in
- **Completed Milestones:** Achievements unlocked
- **Weekly Hours:** Time spent learning this week

**For Trainers/Coaches:**
- **Total Students:** Number of students under your supervision
- **Active Programs:** Programs you're managing
- **Evaluations:** Completed and pending assessments
- **Success Rate:** Overall completion rate of your students

**For Administrators:**
- **Total Beneficiaries:** All registered users in your organization
- **Active Programs:** Currently running training programs
- **System Usage:** Platform engagement metrics
- **Reports Generated:** Analytics and progress reports

### Recent Activity Feed

Shows your latest activities:
- Program enrollments
- Milestone completions
- New evaluations
- Coach notes and feedback
- System notifications

### Quick Actions Panel

**Common actions available:**
- Add new beneficiary (Admin/Trainer)
- Create new program (Admin/Trainer)
- Schedule evaluation (Trainer)
- View reports (Admin/Trainer)
- Take assessment (Student)
- Message coach (Student)

### Performance Overview

Visual charts showing:
- Progress trends
- Completion rates
- Engagement metrics
- Achievement statistics

## Basic Operations

### Creating Your Profile

1. **Access Profile Settings**
   - Click your name in the top-right corner
   - Select "Profile Settings"

2. **Complete Your Information**
   - Upload profile photo (optional)
   - Add contact information
   - Set preferred language
   - Configure notification preferences

3. **Professional Information**
   - Add skills and interests
   - Set learning goals
   - Choose preferred learning style

### Enrolling in Programs

**For Students:**
1. Go to **Programs** section
2. Browse available programs
3. Click "View Details" for program information
4. Click "Enroll" to join
5. Confirm enrollment and start learning

**Prerequisites Check:**
- Some programs may have prerequisites
- Complete required courses first
- Check skill level requirements

### Taking Assessments

1. **Access Evaluations**
   - Go to "Evaluations" in main menu
   - Or click "Take Assessment" from dashboard

2. **Start Assessment**
   - Read instructions carefully
   - Check time limits
   - Save progress automatically

3. **Submit and Review**
   - Submit when complete
   - View results immediately
   - Download certificates if earned

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use the platform on my mobile device?**
A: Yes! The platform is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the email instructions.

**Q: Can I download my certificates?**
A: Yes, completed program certificates are available in your profile under "Achievements."

**Q: How do I contact my coach?**
A: Use the messaging system in the Coach Notes section or click "Message Coach" on your dashboard.

**Q: What if I'm having technical problems?**
A: Try refreshing your browser first. If issues persist, contact technical support with details about the problem.
